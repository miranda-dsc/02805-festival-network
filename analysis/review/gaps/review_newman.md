## Actionable, ranked

1. **B3 is not closed, and the hand checks are single-reader.** `second_reader.csv` has 20 rows with blank labels. Until someone reads them blind, "hand-labeled sample" in the paper means one reader, and the origin audit (30 + 50 + 18 + 10 labels) has no agreement figure. A group member (a person, not an agent) should fill the 20 and the exporter then reports kappa between readers. If nobody has time, the paper must say "labelled by one reader" and drop any kappa claim for the origin audit.

2. **Item 4's headline terms are not robust, and the text blurs that.** The family-wise check (max-gap) passes for zero terms: `p_max` is below 0.05 for 0 of 649, and the best is 0.14 (albums). Only the BH list passes, and that list is mostly generic words (dj, signed, based, radio, festival, label, bbc, eps, school, piece, sean). The text says "British, England and London ... passed that check" right after introducing the max-gap test, so a reader takes it for the stricter one. Say in one sentence that nothing passes the max-gap test, and that the BH terms are exploratory. Do not carry the term list into the paper as a result; keep it as a descriptive example, or drop it.

3. **The zero-foreign tier comparison in item 4 is selected by construction.** Of 1,856 zero-foreign acts, 231 have an English intro (12%), against 1,153 of 1,153 in the 10+ tier. Zero-foreign acts with an English article are the ones whose only article is the English one, and 165 of those are GB. So the contrast is "English-only article" against "English plus 10 or more languages", which is partly an article-length and editorial-attention difference, not reach. The misses sentence names "editorial coverage" but not this selection; add it, with the 12% against 100% figures. "compared with all 1,153 acts in the highest tier" is unclear; write "1,153 of 1,153".

4. **Per-year consistency for the sound effect is thinner than "positive in all nine years" suggests.** In `sound_effect.csv` the 2024 estimate is 0.63 per 0.10 and its festival-deletion range runs from -0.08 to 1.51, and 2017 and 2025 start at 0.40 and 0.45. The pooled deletion range 0.96 to 1.82 hides this. Report the yearly range (0.63 to 2.25), and note 2024 can lose its sign under one festival deletion.

5. **Item 1's check is an agreement between two derived measures, not independent of each other.** Both Wikipedia TF-IDF and the tag cosine are computed on the same acts, and the null fixes the observed tag scores and only shuffles Wikipedia-covered act placement. That is a fair test of "agreement beyond lineup structure" but the sentence "captures agreement between two text measures" could be read as validation of tags. Add that the Wikipedia measure shares genre words with the tags, so agreement is expected; the finding is that it holds after the null, nothing stronger. Also the 200-draw p floor of 0.005 is hit in all nine years, so the p values carry no resolution; say "above all 200 draws" and not p.

6. **Smaller points in `findings_reach.md`.**
   - The zero-foreign tier is 1,550 no-article acts plus 306 with a home-language article, so the tier is mostly Wikipedia coverage. The leakage test (22% and 45% reassigned, difference of r excess falling from 0.062 to 0.008 and 0.017) shows the zero-minus-one contrast can vanish; the sentence "zero-foreign excess stayed above +0.21" is true but the contrast between tiers is the claim the paper would make, and it is not robust. State the claim as "zero and one foreign tiers are alike at +0.2, both above the 10+ tier at +0.01".
   - The 10+ tier is inside its null band "under all three rules" but 5 of 9 years above null; give the year count next to the in-band claim.
   - The Q-id audit has 29 of 30 correct but 4 ambiguous and no alias in the sample, so precision by match method is not estimated; the text says so.
   - Lineup size in `edition_coverage.csv` reaches 2,194 acts (median 162); one edition looks like a festival-wide list. Check it is a single edition and not a merged page, since it dominates pair statistics weighted by shared acts.

7. **Story fit.** Five of the new analyses (term test, tag agreement, origin audit, reach tiers, circuits) are now more than the paper holds in six pages. The two or three that carry the story: reach tier (the 10+ tier shows no country effect, in-band under three rules) and tag agreement. The origin audit and the term test are Methods or supplement material.
