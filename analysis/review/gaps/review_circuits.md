## Actionable, ranked

1. **National share (item 1) is partly festival loyalty, not country.** `national_share` counts editions, so an act that plays festival A in 2015, 2016 and 2018 and festival B once scores 0.75 on one site alone. The within-year null scatters those repeat visits across festivals. For reach-2 acts, 881 of 3,144 have three or more editions. Restricting to acts with exactly 2 editions (2,263 acts, no repeat possible): observed 0.777 vs null 0.734, excess +0.044, against the reported +0.082 for the whole class. About half of the headline excess in the 2-festival row is loyalty. Fix: do the share on exactly-k-edition acts, or count each festival once per act on both sides, and report which version the paper uses. The 3 to 4 and 5+ rows need the same treatment (they are the ones loyalty inflates most).

2. **No take-home sentence, and the docstring hypothesis and the numbers pull in different directions.** The docstring asks whether small acts carry the country effect. Results: absolute gap grows with reach (+0.45, +0.93, +1.33; 49% of the gap from 5+ acts), while the tilt falls (2.15, 1.92, 1.47). That is mostly the k(k-1)/2 point she already makes, but `findings.md` never says what it means for the story (watts: the untagged circuit carries the country effect). Add one sentence that states the reading, and say whether it agrees with `analysis/watts/tagged.py`. A second method for the reach definition is missing: cross-tab reach class against tagged and untagged (a few lines, tag file exists) so reach is shown to track "small" and is not just asserted. Item 2 also still has no check on the claim "which acts carry it" beyond the null.

3. **The outside-GB numbers rest on too little.** 57 pairs in total, by year 4, 9, 14, 13 (2016 to 2019), 3, 6, 7, 1 (2022 to 2025); 2025 has a single pair, so "8/8 years positive" is nearly empty for the later years. The 6.92x is 80 observed against 11.6 expected shared acts; the 0.60x for 5+ is 52 against 87. The pairs are concentrated in two clusters (Pukkelpop, Rock Werchter, Les Ardentes, Graspop, Tomorrowland weekend 1; Primavera, Mad Cool, Vida, Benicassim), so festival-level resampling would be far wider than the counts suggest. Item 3 reports no deletion range for these ratios and item 4 reports gaps without their deletion ranges (from the csv: 5+ abroad is +0.27 with -0.21 to +0.51 after deletions, so the sign is not stable). Either give the ranges and the observed/expected counts, or drop the abroad ratios from the findings and keep the one line that the UK is not the whole story at 2 and 3 to 4 festivals.

4. **The "1 festival" rows are vacuous.** An act with reach 1 cannot be shared by two different festivals, so every pair-level number is 0 by construction ("+0.00, positive in 0 of 9 years", "0%"). Drop the class from items 2 and 4 (keep it only for the 82% count). Same for `years` of 8 vs 9 in the abroad rows: say once that years without a same-country-abroad pair are skipped.

5. **Item 3 reads ambiguously.** "which the absolute gaps in 2 hide" means item 2, not 2 festivals. The ratio-of-ratios divides by a different-country ratio that is below 1 (0.63, 0.71, 0.85: the null puts more shared acts across countries than observed), so part of the tilt is the different-country deficit; one clause saying so keeps it honest. Notation for years is mixed ("9 of 9" and "0/8").
