# Methods number sources

- **Clashfinder licence and request delay.** CC BY-NC 3.0 was confirmed in the footer of the [Glastonbury 2025 schedule](https://clashfinder.com/s/g2025/), an edition in `data/clashfinder_ids.txt`. The two-second configured pause came from `src/00_download_clashfinder.py`. The ten-second directive came from the [Clashfinder robots file](https://clashfinder.com/robots.txt), as retrieved in `analysis/review/gaps/review_euler_refs.md`. Request logs and `data/raw/clashfinder/` were absent locally. This comparison described the configured pause, and actual request timing remained unverified.
- **Selection and size.** The 2015 to 2025 window, 30-act minimum and four-year threshold came from `src/00a_find_festivals.py`. The 2020 to 2021 exclusions came from `analysis/common.py`. The 214 editions, 44 festivals, 53,925 bookings, 32,293 act keys and 20 HTML-affected keys came from the loader audit in `analysis/review/gaps/granovetter.md`.
- **MusicBrainz dates and coverage.** The `TIMESTAMP` member of `data/raw/musicbrainz/dump/artist.tar.xz` recorded 21 November 2026 at 01:17 UTC. The archive mtime was 22 November at 22:54 Copenhagen time. The separate API pilot files `data/raw/musicbrainz/acts.json` and `data/raw/musicbrainz/run.log` were dated 22 November at 22:48 Copenhagen time. The tag analysis used the dump. The three-vote threshold came from `src/03b_musicbrainz_dump.py`. The 35% median strict tag coverage came from `analysis/watts/tag_coverage.csv` and `analysis/watts/findings.md`.
- **Wikipedia collection and cohort.** The JSON cache mtimes under `data/raw/wikipedia/` ranged from 00:31 to 00:44 Copenhagen time on 24 November 2026 (23:31 to 23:44 UTC on 23 November), from the sitematrix response to the last query response. These dates describe the local cache. The two-festival minimum, 5,712-act cohort and 662 accepted ambiguous matches came from `analysis/watts/reach/findings_reach.md` and `analysis/watts/reach/analyze.py`. Tiers 0, 1, 2 to 4, 5 to 9 and 10 or more came from `analysis/watts/reach/reach_tiers.py`.
- **Figure and draws.** The distinct-festival national share for Fig. 2 came from `analysis/watts/reach/findings_reach.md` and `analysis/watts/reach/reach_null.py`. The 2,000 yearly draws per tier came from `analysis/watts/tagged.py` and `analysis/watts/reach/reach_null.py`. The 9,999 full-lineup draws came from `analysis/watts/run.py`. The 200 yearly text agreement draws and lineup-size thirds came from `analysis/newman/wikitext/run.py`. [Curveball reference](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms5114)
- **Hand checks for Results.** The MusicBrainz counts 39/40, 19/20 and 17/22 came from `analysis/review/round3.md`. The Wikidata counts 29/30 and 7/20 came from `analysis/watts/reach/audit.csv` and `analysis/watts/reach/miss_audit.csv`. The 95% Wilson intervals were recomputed from 17/22 (56.6% to 89.9%) and 29/30 (83.3% to 99.4%). The audit files and reviews documented single-reader checks. The separate origin audit's `analysis/newman/wikitext/second_reader.csv` still had empty labels.
- **Sensitivity and text results.** The strict-match excesses +0.239 and -0.002, leakage settings 22% and 45%, and zero-minus-one contrasts 0.062, 0.008 and 0.017 came from `analysis/watts/reach/findings_reach.md`. The 28% median intro coverage and +0.18 to +0.40 yearly agreement excess came from `analysis/newman/wikitext/findings.md`.
