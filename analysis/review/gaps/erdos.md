The absolute gap measures excess shared acts per same-country pair minus excess per cross-country pair, with each year weighted equally. It grows from +0.45 to +0.93 to +1.33 across the repeat-festival bins. The 5+ bin supplies 49% of the gap overall, with +1.46 for GB pairs and +0.27 for same-country pairs elsewhere, each against cross-country pairs. An act booked at more editions in a year can contribute to more pairs. With Wikipedia bins and the full-lineup shuffle, the gap is +1.51 for zero foreign and -0.58 for 10+ foreign. The 10+ tier has fewer shared acts than expected in same-country pairs (0.77 times expected) and more across countries (1.12 times expected). Yearly act degrees fix the total overlap weight before same-brand pairs are excluded, so shifts in within-country and cross-country overlap trade off within each tier.
