### Methods facts for the paper

- **Source and licence.** User-entered Clashfinder event JSON, one Clashfinder ID per edition. Flatten stages and events to show rows, retain any supplied MusicBrainz artist ID, and take edition date and year from the earliest scheduled show. Clashfinder publishes lineups under CC BY-NC 3.0. See `src/00_download_clashfinder.py:29`, `src/01_load_clashfinder.py:21` and the [Clashfinder licence notice](https://clashfinder.com/s/nos19/).
- **Selection.** Use dated entries in 2015 to 2025 with at least 30 listed acts. Normalize festival names and keep the largest Clashfinder per festival-year. Require at least four qualifying years. The exclusion list removes named non-European and non-music events and Tomorrowland Weekend 2. The four-year and 30-act rules apply before cleaning and analysis exclusions. See `src/00a_find_festivals.py:12`, lines 37 to 62 and `data/clashfinder_ids.txt`.
- **Cleaning.** Exclude non-music stages by keyword with exceptions, placeholder or empty act keys, and year-bracketed screenings outside 2020 and 2021, with an exception for Sunday (1994). Flag pandemic rebroadcasts. Keep one booking per edition and act key, preferring rows with an MBID. For the main sample drop rebroadcasts, Leeds and 2020 to 2021. Use venue country. Keep Tomorrowland Winter and Weekend 1 as separate editions and remove their same-brand pairs from the main overlap and reach statistics. See `src/02_clean_lineups.py:60`, `analysis/common.py:13` and `:39`, and `analysis/watts/reach/reach_null.py:96`.
- **Act identity.** The unit is the normalized billing key. Lowercase, strip accents, remove bracketed notes where a name remains, strip trailing live or DJ labels and leading The, replace ampersands with 'and', and collapse punctuation and spaces. Homonyms can merge and spelling or billing variants can split. Include the HTML limitation if results use the current keys. See `src/02_clean_lineups.py:42`, `analysis/common.py:31`, `analysis/euler/findings.md:12` and the Majestic example in `analysis/watts/reach/findings_reach.md:69`.
- **MusicBrainz.** The artist dump archive records 21 Nov 2026 at 01:17 UTC in its TIMESTAMP member. Match by one supplied MBID, then unique normalized primary name, then unique alias. Set conflicting supplied IDs aside. Flag the highest-vote ambiguous candidate and require three tag plus rating votes in total. Name precision was 39/40 (97.5%, Wilson 95% interval 87% to 100%), alias 19/20 (95%, 76% to 99%), and ambiguous 21/40 (52.5%, 37% to 67%) before the filter and 17/22 (77%) after it. The audit counts unsure as unconfirmed and mostly judges name and alias by fit. Use strict ID, name and alias for the main tag analysis. Use genre counts where present and tag counts otherwise, keep positive weights and normalize each tagged act to equal total weight. Coverage is the share of booked acts with usable tags per edition. The dump gives present-day tags for past lineups. See `data/raw/musicbrainz/dump/artist.tar.xz` (TIMESTAMP), `CLAUDE.md:158`, `src/03b_musicbrainz_dump.py:34`, `:69`, `analysis/review/round3.md`, and `analysis/watts/genre_profiles.py:8`.
- **Wikidata and reach.** Start with the 5,712 acts booked at two or more distinct festivals. Join MusicBrainz artist MBIDs to artist dump URL relations, resolve a single Wikidata Q-id with redirects and count open language Wikipedia sitelinks. Leave multiple Q-ids unresolved and check direct Wikipedia URLs when sitelinks are unavailable. Keep unmatched MBIDs and unknown or unmapped artist countries in separate bins. Count editions outside MusicBrainz artist country's nationally official, de facto official and regionally official Babel languages. Simple English shares English home status. Use bins of 0, 1, 2 to 4, 5 to 9 and 10 or more. Reach includes accepted ambiguous matches, with a strict-match sensitivity. The Q-id audit found 29/30 correct (96.7%, Wilson 83.3% to 99.4%). The miss audit recovered articles for 7/20 sampled no-article acts, with foreign articles for 4/18 sampled zero-foreign acts. See `src/04_wikipedia.py:77`, `:110`, `:194`, `analysis/watts/reach/analyze.py:34`, `reach_tiers.py:15` in the same folder and `findings_reach.md:69` in the same folder.
- **Null and draws.** Curveball trades acts between lineups within year, fixing each edition's lineup size and each act's booking count. For reach, run 2,000 draws per year and group from a shared post-burn-in state. Project onto editions weighted by shared acts, sum yearly country mixing matrices for pooled assortativity and use the central 95% of pooled null draws. The full-lineup Watts baseline uses 9,999 draws per year and its tagged and untagged subset nulls use 2,000. Circuits uses 200 per year. The Wikitext tag-agreement check uses 200 per year within lineup-size thirds among Wikipedia-covered acts, with observed tag scores held fixed. Its overlap regression uses the 9,999-draw Watts expectations. See `analysis/watts/reach/reach_null.py:15`, `analysis/watts/reach/findings_reach.md:23`, `analysis/watts/run.py:17`, `analysis/watts/tagged.py:12`, `analysis/circuits/findings.md:1` and `analysis/newman/wikitext/findings.md:3`.
- **Deletions.** Drop one festival across all its editions at a time. For reach assortativity, remove its edges from observed and each existing shuffled projection and recompute the excess over the null mean. For the reach regression and Wikitext overlap regression, drop its pairs and refit with the original pair expectations. Circuits also keeps the pair null fixed. Newman network metrics rebuild the observed graphs and refit Louvain after removal. If reporting nestedness, give its separate protocol of 400 full draws and 200 new draws per affected year after each festival deletion. See `analysis/watts/reach/findings_reach.md:23`, `analysis/newman/wikitext/findings.md:5`, `analysis/circuits/findings.md:10`, `analysis/newman/findings.md:3` and `analysis/kleinberg/nested/findings.md:3`.
