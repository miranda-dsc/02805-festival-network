## Wrong or overreaching, ranked

1. **The 10 or more tier is not "shared across borders at the rate the null gives".** (Significance statement, line 21; Discussion, line 123.) Under the shared null the same-country observed to expected ratio in that tier is 0.77 and the cross-country ratio is 1.12, the gap is -0.58 (`comparison.csv`). The assortativity excess is +0.007 inside its band, positive in 4 of 9 years. That is no country excess, not a null rate of crossing borders.
   - Line 21, replace "Acts with Wikipedia articles in ten or more foreign languages are booked across borders at the rate a random shuffle gives." with "Acts with Wikipedia articles in ten or more foreign languages show no country pattern beyond the shuffle."
   - Line 123, replace "Acts with articles in ten or more foreign languages are shared across borders at the rate the null gives." with "Acts with articles in ten or more foreign languages show no country assortativity beyond the null (+0.007, inside its band); their same country pairs share fewer acts than the null gives (0.77 times) and their cross country pairs more (1.12 times)."

2. **Abstract, "+0.32" is wrong.** (Line 30.) The value is 0.3148, which is +0.31; the Results (line 70) correctly say +0.315. Replace "falls from $+0.32$ for acts at two festivals" with "falls from $+0.31$ for acts at two festivals".

3. **Abstract describes the country contrast as a share beyond the null.** (Line 30.) The 2.52 is the same country minus cross country difference in shared acts per pair, minus what the null gives for that difference (4.44 minus 1.92). Replace "Festivals in the same country share 2.52 more acts per pair than the null predicts, in each of nine years" with "Festivals in the same country share 2.52 more acts per pair than festivals in different countries, beyond what the null gives, in each of nine years".

4. **Sound section mixes two match policies.** (Line 89.) The +1.36 is strict matching; the +2.05 (+1.68 to +2.48) is the with ambiguous matches row of `watts/findings.md`. The strict row is +2.04 (+1.66 to +2.47, 9 of 9). Replace "the same country gap is $+2.05$ acts at equal genre similarity ($+1.68$ to $+2.48$ after deletions, nine of nine years)" with "the same country gap is $+2.04$ acts at equal genre similarity ($+1.66$ to $+2.47$ after deletions, nine of nine years)".

5. **The Wikipedia agreement null is misdescribed.** (Line 91.) It does not shuffle which acts have articles. It trades Wikipedia covered acts between lineups within year and lineup size thirds, with each edition's number of covered acts and the tag similarities fixed (`newman/wikitext/findings.md`, item 1). Replace "above a null that shuffles which acts have articles" with "above a null that trades the covered acts between lineups within each year and lineup size third".

6. **"Significance statement" says eleven years.** (Line 21.) The data are nine years (2015 to 2019 and 2022 to 2025) within an eleven year window. Replace "We use eleven years of European lineups" with "We use nine years of European lineups, 2015 to 2025 without 2020 and 2021".

7. **The subsection title "Little known acts carry the tilt within every reach class" claims a share.** (Line 83.) The result is the ratio of same country to cross country excess within tag status; the circuits review and `CLAUDE.md` say not to claim a share of the gap, and untagged is a proxy for obscure that the text itself qualifies. Replace the title with "Untagged acts tilt toward their country within every reach class".

8. **The "practical consequence" paragraph is an untested inference.** (Line 125.) Nothing in the paper measures a homogeneity index built on raw overlap. Replace the paragraph with: "This bears on how festival similarity is measured. A raw count of shared acts is dominated by widely booked acts, because each of them links many pairs: acts at five or more festivals carry 49% of the raw same country gap while having the weakest tilt. A similarity measure built on raw overlap would therefore weight the international circuit most. We did not build such a measure, so this is an expectation, not a result."

The outside Britain ratios (6.92, 3.07, 0.60; 1.00 without Belgium) are in `granovetter/circuits_check/` but not in the paper.
